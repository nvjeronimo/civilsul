Mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is built like a well-made Algarve house: lime-washed walls as ground, the painted barra (base band) as the foot of every section, the platibanda (parapet) as its crown. It is literally what Civilsul builds and rebuilds, and it is in their own photos (Monte do Pocinho's blue barra, Vale del Rey's white volumes). Architectural and restrained: the grammar is structural, never a postcard. Refuses the hero-with-overlay template and the touristy "azulejo + sun" cliché.
OWN-WORLD: Ground limewash #F5F6F4 with an extremely subtle mineral texture (CSS noise at ≤3% or none), ink #1B1B1B. Barras: Algarve blue #1F4FA0 is the primary barra (carries the CTA and the facts), ochre #D9A43A a secondary barra used once or twice, terracotta #B5553A only as a hairline/roof-tile accent. Each major section sits on its barra: a full-width painted band 40–72px tall at the section foot (content may sit inside it: facts, links). Section tops can carry a platibanda profile: a simple stepped or softly curved parapet silhouette cut in the section's top edge (CSS clip-path with few points or an SVG with <8 nodes, drawn once and reused — it is architecture, not decoration). Type: Manrope is NOT allowed (used by C). Use Schibsted Grotesk Variable for display (700–800, confident, tracking ≥ -0.035em) and Sofia Sans Variable for body; tabular numerals. Photos large, square-cornered, sitting on the wall like windows (deep, generous white margins), never cards. Buttons: rectangular, blue barra fill with lime-white text, or ink outline.
STORY: The visitor recognises the house they want (white walls, clean lines, real finished work), reads a plain promise, sees the six services and the works like walking past houses, reads the company facts on the blue barra, and calls or messages.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/12 on limewash: header strip (logo, nav, phone + note, "Pedir orçamento"), H1 "Construímos, reconstruímos e remodelamos no Algarve desde 1985." at ~4.5–5rem, one support line. Right 7/12: large photo of Monte do Pocinho (blue barra house) set like a window in the wall. Across the full width at the bottom of the viewport: the blue barra (~88px) carrying the primary action "Pedir orçamento", WhatsApp, and the facts (Desde 1985 · Alvará nº 4511 · Quarteira, Algarve). Mobile: H1, photo, then the barra with CTA stacked; sticky bar.
