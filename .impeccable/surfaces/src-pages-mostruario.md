## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is a builder's material sample board (mostruário) from a good showroom: every service is a physical sample chip cut from Civilsul's own finished works (the timber cladding, the pool water, the terracotta roof, the lime-white wall, the patterned floor tile, the blue facade), each with a small printed tag; the sample opens into the finished house. The proof is material, not adjectives. It refuses the category hero-with-overlay and the icon-card service grid.
OWN-WORLD: Two grounds that alternate by section: charcoal mounting board #1C1E1D (first viewport, the sample wall, footer) and lime white #F6F6F3 (reading sections: works, company, process, quote). Chips are square or 4:5 photographic swatches with a 1px hairline (#ffffff22 on charcoal, #1c1e1d1f on lime) and a tag strip beneath: "Ref. S-01" code, material/finish line, "Aplicado em" + work title link. Swatches are CROPS of the real photos done in CSS (the same Picture with object-position + scale), never new rasters. Accent: the logo's yellow #FFE500 only as a small round sticker dot (the logo's circle) marking the selected/active sample and the primary action; the logo blue #0B84CF (text use #0A6DAD on lime for AA) for links and focus. Type: Schibsted Grotesk (variable, @fontsource-variable/schibsted-grotesk) for everything: display 700–800 at confident scale (hero ~4.5–5.5rem desktop, tracking ≥ -0.035em), tags 500 at 0.8125–0.875rem with tabular numerals; no monospace, no serif, no uppercase eyebrow labels (tags are the only small type, and they sit BELOW the thing they label). Corners square. No shadows except the lifted chip on hover/active (soft, offset).
STORY: The visitor sees, in one glance, a wall of real material samples and a plain promise; touches a sample and the whole finished house appears; walks through the six services as sample families, each with its chips and the works they came from; sees the full works as large photographs; reads the company facts (since 1985, alvará 4511, Quarteira, Algarve + rest of country) as one clean spec tag; then "monta a sua amostra" in the quote form and sends it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Charcoal board full height. Left ~5/12: logo + nav strip at top (Serviços, Obras, Empresa, Contactos, phone with call note, "Pedir orçamento" button in lime white with the yellow dot); H1 "Construímos, reconstruímos e remodelamos no Algarve desde 1985." in lime white; one support line; primary "Pedir orçamento" + secondary WhatsApp. Right ~7/12: a 3×2 grid of six sample chips (one per service, each cropped tight from a different real work photo), each with its tag; the first carries the yellow sticker dot. Mobile: H1 first, then a horizontally scrollable row of chips (scroll-snap), sticky bottom bar Ligar · WhatsApp · Orçamento.
FORM: Mostruário de materiais, grounded candidate 5 of 7 in re-roll round 1 (seed key 42e80edc, --reroll 1).
SIGNATURE: sample → house. Hovering or focusing a chip (tap on touch) animates the crop out to the full photograph inside the chip's frame (scale and object-position transition, ~700ms expo-out) and the tag swaps to the work title; on the services page each family's lead chip can expand into a wide plate. On the works index, the grid is a sample wall: each work shows its tight crop first and resolves to the full photo as it enters the viewport (once). Reduced motion: full photos, no transitions. The quote form's type-of-work options are sample chips with crops; the running summary is a single sample tag ("Ref. do pedido", fields as tag lines).
