# Surface: Civilsul · variante "Aviso de Obra" (src/pages/aviso, src/variants/aviso)

Mode: Persuade. Audience, job, proof and constraints as in PRODUCT.md (owners in the Algarve, mostly on phones; trust → qualified quote request; facts only; PT + EN; static; CSP script-src 'self').

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every page is the building-permit board (aviso de obra / placa de licença) that hangs on every construction site in Portugal: a white enamel panel, bolted to the hoarding, whose legal fields are filled with Civilsul's real facts. It refuses soft marketing prose and the stock hero-with-overlay.
OWN-WORLD: Ground = site hoarding: light galvanised grey #DCDEDA with a faint vertical corrugation rhythm (repeating gradient, very subtle). Content lives on white #FFFFFF enamel panels with a 2px ink border, four drawn screw heads in the corners, square corners. Ink #111111. Field grammar: small uppercase field label (Overpass 700, tracked +0.04em, it is a legal label not an eyebrow) above a large value. Brand blue #0B84CF as the panel header band on key boards (like municipal boards) and for links. Safety yellow #FFD400 with black 45° hazard stripes reserved for the primary action and the "work in progress" moments only. Photos are printed site banners (lona) stretched full-bleed on the hoarding behind the panels. Type: Overpass (the highway-signage face) 800–900 for display, Overpass 400/600 body, tabular numerals. Icons: authored SVG, 2px stroke, square caps, pictogram style like site safety signs.
STORY: The visitor reads the board like a passer-by reads a real obra: who builds (Civilsul), under what permit (4511), since when (1985), where (Quarteira, Algarve), what (the six services); walks along the hoarding of finished works; then fills the blank board of their own obra (the quote form) and sends it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed banner photo (Vale del Rey house) on the hoarding. Bolted over it, left-of-centre, a large white Aviso panel (~560px wide desktop) with a blue header band "Civilsul · Construtora do Sul, Lda.", then H1 "Construímos no Algarve desde 1985." in Overpass 900, then field rows: Alvará nº 4511 · Sede Quarteira, Algarve · Obras moradias, reconstrução, remodelações, piscinas, obras públicas · Telefone 912 276 607 (with call note). Primary action "Pedir orçamento" as a yellow/black hazard-striped plate; secondary WhatsApp. Header: a narrow white strip bolted at the top (logo, nav, PT/EN, phone). Mobile: photo band 45vh, the board overlaps it from below, sticky bottom action bar.
FORM: Aviso de Obra, grounded candidate 1 of 7 (Impeccable's pick), chosen by the user alongside the roll. Seed key 42e80edc.
SIGNATURE: boards are bolted on: on first view each panel settles with a short drop and its screw heads rotate a quarter turn (once, transform only, reduced-motion = static). The quote page is a blank licence board whose fields fill as the visitor types; the last step shows the completed board ready to send. Works index = a long hoarding of banner photos with small bolted caption plates.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
