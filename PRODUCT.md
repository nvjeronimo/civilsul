## Users
- **Private owners in the Algarve** (Portuguese and foreign residents, many English-speaking) who want to build, rebuild, extend or renovate a house or apartment, add a pool or redo exterior works. They usually research on a phone, compare a few builders and want to know if this one is serious before calling.
- **Companies and investors** renovating apartments or buildings for tourism and short-term rental (portfolio includes TOPAZMOMENT, MAISON AMARANDE, Portaldegénios, Lda., an agrotourism in Monte do Pocinho).
- **Public entities** (the company states it executes public works).

## Product Purpose
Civilsul (Construtora do Sul, Lda.) is a civil construction company in Quarteira, Algarve, working since 1985 under construction permit (alvará) nº 4511. The site must turn a visitor into a qualified quote request (type of work, location, timing, photos) by proving competence with real finished works. The current site hides the contacts inside a banner image, has no form, no project pages and almost no text for search engines.

## Positioning

## Product Principles
1. Show the work: real finished houses carry the trust, not adjectives.
2. Contact in one tap: phone, WhatsApp and email visible everywhere, never inside an image.
3. Qualify the request: the form collects what a builder needs to answer (type, place, timing, photos) so the first call is useful.
4. Be found locally: one clear page per service and per work, in PT and EN, with real text.
5. Say only what is true: 1985, alvará 4511, the service list and the photos are the proof.
